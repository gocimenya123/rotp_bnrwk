# Boku no Rhythm wo Kiitekure - Ripples of the Past addon

Adds the Stand **Boku no Rhythm wo Kiitekure** (user: Oyecomova, JoJo Part 7: Steel Ball Run) to
[Ripples of the Past](https://github.com/StandoByte/Ripples-of-the-Past) for Minecraft 1.16.5 / Forge.
Obtainable from a Stand Arrow like any other Stand (`randomWeight(1)`).

| Slot | Action | What it does |
|------|--------|--------------|
| LMB  | Punch | Standard RotP melee |
| Ability 1 | **Plant Bomb** | Touch an entity, dropped item, block or water/lava surface. The clock pin springs up and explodes after 5 s (18 dmg, r=3.2, knockback). Max 8 planted at once. |
| Ability 2 | **Bomb Throw** | 6 pins in a shotgun spread; they stick to what they hit and each blows up after 5 s (8 dmg, r=2.4). |
| Ability 3 | **Smoke Bomb** | Smoke cloud with 14 tiny pins hidden inside, detonating one by one over ~2-5 s (4.5 dmg each, r=1.9). |
| Ability 4 | **Item Bomb** | Toss an item from your hand with a pin in it. |
| Ability 5 | **Entity Bomb** | Plants a pin on the mob / player / item you look at, up to 3.5 blocks away (8 dmg, r=2.2) (walls block it). |
| Ability 6 | Block | Standard RotP block |

The actions sit on RotP's ability hotbar, so the actual keys are whatever you bound for "stand ability" / hotbar
cycling in RotP's controls. Abilities unlock with resolve level 0 / 1 / 2.

Explosions use RotP's `StandEntityDamageSource` while the user has a manifested Stand (plain explosion damage
otherwise), never hurt the user or their Stand, and only carve soft blocks (hardness <= 0.8) when mobGriefing is on.

## Build
1. Run `setup_wrapper.bat` once (needs any Gradle on PATH) - it creates gradlew, gradlew.bat and the wrapper jar for Gradle 7.6.4.
2. `./gradlew build`  (Java 8 toolchain). Run configs: `./gradlew runClient`.
3. RotP version is pinned in `gradle.properties` (`main_mod_version`) - same value as the Killer Queen addon.

## Textures / model
`tools/gen_textures.py` generates *placeholder* textures (stand skin with stitches, clock pin, icons).
Replace them with real art; UV layout is documented at the top of that script.
The stand model (`BnrwkModel`) is generated by `tools/gen_stand.py` (`--preview` renders `tools/stand_preview.png`). It extends RotP's `HumanoidStandModel` and adds a long crow beak with open jaw, black face mask with red eyes, a tilted golden crown disc, side discs and a big hunched pink-purple cloak, matching the reference art. The previous shoebill version is kept in `tools/gen_stand_old_shoebill.py`. RotP uses its own model system, not GeckoLib, so GeckoLib is not a dependency.

## Bomb size & timing
All big pins have a 5 s fuse. The blast scales with the size of what the pin is stuck to (player = x1, cube root of the
bounding-box volume, x0.7 .. x2.5; radius capped at 8): a cow hits harder than a chicken, an iron golem about twice as hard as a player.

## Holding bombs (any player, no Stand needed)
* **Pins in the world:** right-click a big pin and *keep the right mouse button pressed* - its fuse is held back for as
  long as you hold (and stay within 12 blocks). Release and it ticks again.
* **Pin on yourself:** with both hands empty, hold the right mouse button.
* **Bombed items:** pick one up and the pin goes into the item and **keeps ticking in your inventory** - if it runs out
  the item explodes on you. Hold the item in your hand (selected slot or off hand) with the right mouse button pressed to
  freeze the fuse; release to let it run again.
* **Dropping** the item (Q, death) puts the pin back on the dropped item with the time that was left.
* **Placing** a bombed block leaves the pin stuck on the placed block.
* A pin belongs to a single item: piles of 2+ with a pin can't be picked up. Items left in chests keep their absolute
  countdown (they get 2 s of grace when taken out overdue).

## Stand model
`tools/gen_stand.py` is the single source for the Stand model (`BnrwkModel.java` is generated) and its skin.
`python3 tools/gen_stand.py --preview` renders `tools/stand_preview.png` (front / 3-4 / side / back).

## Sounds
`tools/gen_sounds.py` synthesizes every sound (needs numpy, scipy, ffmpeg) into `assets/rotp_bnrwk/sounds/*.ogg`; the
mapping is `sounds.json`, the events are in `InitSounds`. Theme: the Stand is a clock - ticks, ratchets, a spring and brass.
* **Summon** - clockwork winding up + chime (played from `BnrwkEntity.onAddedToWorld`).
* **Barrage** - a burst of fast tick-tock every 0.5 s while the fists fly (`BnrwkBarrage`).
* **Bombs** - tick / tock alternate; the rhythm speeds up as the fuse burns (1 s -> 0.5 s -> 0.25 s), then the pin springs
  up and the blast adds a metallic layer on top of the vanilla explosion.
* Plant / Entity / Item Bomb, Bomb Throw and Smoke Bomb each have their own cue.
No official audio exists for this Stand and none is bundled: any file can be replaced by a recording with the same name.

## Barrage
While the barrage pose is active `BnrwkModel.setupAnim` zeroes the rotation of `body` and `head`, so the cloak, bill and gear
stay still and only the arms move.
